Documents Needed

Required Documents

Prepare these documents for a smooth and fast visa application

Valid Passport

Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your travel dates. Minimum 2 blank pages required.

Passport-Sized Photos

2 recent colour photos with white background. Must be clear and meet Saudi visa photo specifications.

Certificate of Islam

A letter from your local mosque confirming you are a practising Muslim. Required for Umrah and Hajj visas.

Vaccination Certificates

Meningitis ACWY vaccine is mandatory. COVID-19 vaccination may also be required. Yellow fever if travelling from affected regions.

Flight Booking

Confirmed return flight tickets or booking confirmation. Our team can arrange this as part of your package.

Hotel Confirmation

Confirmed hotel accommodation in Makkah and Madinah. Included as standard with all AlMarwaTravel packages.

Mahram Documentation

Women under 45 must travel with a Mahram (male guardian). Marriage certificate or birth certificate may be required.

Proof of Residency

UK residents may need to provide proof of UK address such as a utility bill or bank statement (dated within 3 months).

Common Questions

Visa FAQs

Answers to the most frequently asked visa questions

Can UK residents perform Umrah without a travel agent?
Yes, UK residents can apply for a Saudi Tourist eVisa independently online. However, using an authorised agent like AlMarwaTravel ensures your visa is correctly linked to your accommodation and flights, reducing the risk of issues at the border.
How long does an Umrah visa take to process?
With AlMarwaTravel, most Umrah eVisas are processed within 24–48 hours once all documents are submitted correctly. We recommend applying at least 2 weeks before your travel date to allow for any unexpected delays.
Can a woman travel for Umrah without a Mahram?
Women aged 45 and above can travel for Umrah without a Mahram in an organised group. Women under 45 must be accompanied by a Mahram (male guardian). Saudi authorities have relaxed some rules — contact us for the latest guidance.
Which vaccinations are required for Saudi Arabia?
The Meningitis ACWY vaccine is mandatory for all pilgrims. Yellow fever vaccination is required if travelling from an affected country. We recommend consulting your GP at least 6–8 weeks before departure to ensure all vaccinations are up to date.
Can I extend my Umrah visa while in Saudi Arabia?
Umrah visas are generally not extendable. If you wish to stay longer, you should apply for a Tourist eVisa which allows up to 90 days per visit and is valid for 1 year. Overstaying can result in fines or bans from re-entry.
Is the Hajj visa included in AlMarwaTravel packages?
Yes! All AlMarwaTravel Hajj packages include full visa processing at no additional charge. Our team handles the entire application process on your behalf, including document collection and liaison with Saudi authorities.
What happens if my visa is rejected?
With our 99% success rate, rejections are very rare. If a rejection does occur, we investigate the reason, correct any issues and resubmit at no additional charge. We also provide full refunds if the visa cannot be obtained.
Do I need a Certificate of Islam from a mosque?
Yes, Saudi authorities require a Certificate of Islam (also called a Muslim Declaration) for Umrah and Hajj visas. This is a letter from your local mosque or Islamic centre confirming your faith. We can advise on the exact format required.
