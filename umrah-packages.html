Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an Umrah package from the UK cost?
On an average, 7-night packages start from around £449 for 3-star, £579 for 4-star, and £839 for 5-star, per person. The final price depends on the season, length of stay, hotel and how early you book. Contact us for a live quote for your dates.
What are the new Umrah rules for 2026/27?
The main changes for the 2026/27 are: Umrah visas now expire 90 days after issue if unused; visas are only granted with confirmed Nusuk-verified hotel and transport bookings; digital Nusuk permits are checked at the Kabbah and Rawdah visits must be pre-booked; and tourist, visit and transit visas can now be used for Umrah outside the Hajj window.
Can I apply for the Umrah visa myself?
For the traditional Umrah visa, no. It is arranged through a licensed agency. UK citizens may also be able to use a Saudi tourist eVisa, and we can advise which route is best for you.
How early should I book?
Around 2 to 3 months ahead for off-peak trips, 4 to 6 months for Ramadan, and 4 to 5 months for December and winter holidays. Booking early means better hotels and lower flight prices. Note that the visa itself is issued close to your travel date because of the new 30-day validity rule, booking early secures your price and hotels, and we apply for the visa at the right moment.
