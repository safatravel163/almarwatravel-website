What Is a Shifting Hajj Package?

A shifting Hajj package means that you may stay in more than one accommodation during your time in Makkah.

You may first stay in a hotel before the main Hajj days. During the Hajj period, you may move to another building or property closer to Mina and the other Mashair sites.

After completing the main Hajj rituals, you may return to the first hotel or move to another arranged accommodation. The exact plan depends on the package you choose.

Shifting Hajj packages may offer a more affordable option. They may also provide easier access to Mina, Arafat and Muzdalifah during the busiest days of Hajj.

However, you may need to pack your belongings and change rooms during the journey. This may require more effort, especially for families, elderly pilgrims and people with mobility concerns.

Important: Before booking a shifting Hajj package, ask how many times you will move. You should also confirm whether transport is provided and whether you can leave some of your luggage in the main hotel.

What Is a Non-Shifting Hajj Package?

A non-shifting Hajj package allows you to keep the same main accommodation in Makkah before and after the main Hajj rituals.

You will still travel to Mina, Arafat and Muzdalifah on the required days. However, your main Makkah room normally remains reserved during this period.

This means you can leave most of your luggage and personal belongings in your room. You do not need to move everything to another Makkah property.

Non-shifting Hajj packages can provide more comfort and convenience because they involve fewer accommodation changes. They are often suitable for elderly pilgrims, couples, families with children and people who prefer a simpler travel plan.

A non-shifting package may cost more because the room remains reserved throughout the Hajj period. The total price will also depend on the hotel category, location, room-sharing option and package duration.

Which Hajj Package Is Right for You?

A shifting Hajj package may be suitable if you want to manage your budget and do not mind changing accommodation. It can also suit active pilgrims who are comfortable packing and moving during the journey.

A non-shifting Hajj package may be better if you are travelling with elderly family members, children or someone with limited mobility. It can also suit pilgrims who want to keep one main room and avoid extra accommodation changes.

Neither package is better for everyone. The right option depends on your health, age, budget, travel group and preferred level of comfort.

Before choosing, ask for the complete accommodation plan in writing. Check the property names, room-sharing details, hotel distance, transport plan, meal services and accessibility.

Hajj Package Room-Sharing Options

Hajj packages often offer quad, triple, double and single room choices.

Quad sharing

Quad sharing means four people stay in one room. It is normally one of the more affordable options, but the room may feel busy.

Triple sharing

Triple sharing allows three people to stay together. It can suit friends or a small family group.

Double sharing

Double sharing provides more privacy and space for two people. It normally costs more than triple or quad sharing.

Single room

A single room offers the most privacy, but it can cost much more and may have limited availability during Hajj.

Before booking, confirm the number of beds, room size, storage space and bathroom arrangements.

Do not assume that all hotel rooms offer the same space or facilities.

How to Prepare for Hajj 2027

Early preparation can help make your Hajj journey easier.

First, check that your passport meets the required validity period. Your name and passport details should match your Nusuk profile and travel documents.

Keep printed and digital copies of your passport, flight tickets, hotel information, package confirmation, insurance and prescriptions.

Health and vaccination requirements can change. Check the latest official guidance before departure.

Hajj requires physical effort. Regular walking before travelling may help prepare your body for the journey.

Increase your walking slowly and seek medical advice when needed.

Pack light and carry only useful items. Comfortable footwear, personal medicines, Ihram clothing, unscented toiletries, a power bank and copies of your documents can be helpful.

Always check the latest airline rules before packing liquids, batteries and medicines.
